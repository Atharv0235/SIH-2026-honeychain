What This System Cannot Prove (Apiculture Integrity Boundaries)

Decentralized consensus guarantees historical immutability, not scientific omnipotence.

01

A blockchain proves a record was not changed after anchoring. It does not prove the honey is pure.

If an adulterated honey syrup was certified and sealed by a dishonest actor offline, the blockchain merely preserves that fraudulent assertion permanently. Our defense relies on combining physical tamper seals with hardware telemetry and independent lab hash validation.

02

Hive weight shows nectar flow, not floral source.

Rapid hive weight gains demonstrate healthy colony foraging velocity and super filling, but botanical and floral origin (e.g. Mustard vs. Wild Acacia vs. Multifloral) requires accredited microscopic melissopalynology pollen profiling linked in the lab manifest.

03

Health thresholds are prototype rules.

Brood core temperature (34.5°C ± 1.5°C) and acoustic swarm detection frequencies are tuned to standard Apis cerana indica and Apis mellifera colony baselines. Extreme Himalayan cloudbursts or abrupt unseasonal cold fronts can cause transient false anomalies.

04

Prices and revenue splits are estimates.

Fair-trade baseline shares (?480/jar) are indexed to regional cooperative Minimum Support Prices (MSP), local self-help federations, and direct extraction logistics. Dynamic secondary market markups by retail partners remain outside the primary smart contract lock.
