Honest Reading on Apiculture Economics

“At commodity prices the pod costs more than a small apiary can carry. It starts to make sense in three cases: the honey sells verified and direct, the apiary is migratory with high volume, or the pod is shared through a cooperative.”

1. Direct / D2C Enables 3–4x price realization via verifiable QR origin.
2. High-Yield Migratory 500 kg volume amortizes pod overhead down to 7–16%.
3. Cooperative Hub SHG amortizes 1 hub across multiple beekeeper lines.
