SECURITY SPECIFICATION

Being Honest About Time

Offline Timestamp Integrity — An offline record can theoretically be back-dated until anchored, so HoneyChain's consumer interface explicitly denotes:

Phase 1: Pre-Anchored "Recorded locally at [timestamp]"
Phase 2: Post-Anchored "Anchored & Verified at [block timestamp]"

Consumers receive mathematical certainty of the anchoring block height, preventing retroactive batch insertion or fake history fabrication.

RTC Module: DS3231 Monotonic Zero Fabrication Tolerated
