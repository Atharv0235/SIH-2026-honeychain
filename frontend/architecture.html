Anatomy of an Instrumented Hive

Physical Langstroth Assembly

Telescopic Roof & Inner Cover: Weather shield housing passive ventilation gills and low-profile 2W solar replenishment cell.
Honey Super: Upper chamber dedicated to pure surplus comb storage, isolated from the brood via queen excluder screen.
Brood Chamber with SHT31 Probe: Queen laying zone. SHT31 probe continuously logs homeostatic 34.5°C cluster warmth and incubation conditions.
Hive Entrance Add-on: Integrated optical bee activity slot, varroa mite floor inspection grid, and mouse guard damper.
Four Precision Load Cells (4x HX711): Corner-balanced bridge eliminates frame center-of-gravity shifts; records nectar influx & honey reserves with 5g resolution.
Vibration-Damped Concrete Paver: Decouples ground acoustic noise and stabilizes leveling pads against seasonal monsoon soil subsidence.
Compute & Power Specification: ESP32-C3 ultra-low power MCU, HX711 24-bit ADC amplifier, 18650 LiFePO4 battery (8-month field autonomy on 15-min sync intervals).

Why nodes are wireless (ESP-NOW) and the hub is not

Eliminates miles of vulnerable field cabling between boxes that snag on brush or suffer rodent damage, while concentrating power-hungry 4G cellular transmission into a single solar-backed weatherproof base station. Even if cellular connectivity drops during Himalayan monsoons, ESP-NOW peer meshes log uninterrupted locally.
